# Data quality

Three layers of defence, per [ADR 0006](adr/0006-data-architecture.md).

## At ingestion (row level)

`scintille_ingest.validate` runs a Pandera schema generated from the data contract (`FlashRecord`:
ranges, patterns, allowed values) on every parsed file, plus a check that each event time falls
inside its product window (± 5 min). Invalid rows are never dropped silently: they go to
`silver.flashes_quarantine` with the record as JSON and a `reason` listing every failed rule. An
unreadable file gets a `failed` row in `bronze.ingest_log`.

## Overlap between satellites

GOES-East and GOES-West both see the Americas between about 140° W and 70° W, so most flashes there
are recorded twice, once per satellite, with different `flash_id`s (and positions a few km apart).
A static ownership mask gives every point to one instrument: the one with the best viewing angle
among those that see it. For geostationary instruments that is the smallest central angle to the
sub-satellite point, so the GOES-East/West boundary is the meridian halfway between their
sub-points (106.1° W).

- Rule and configuration: `scintille_contract.ownership`. Sub-points come from
  `nominal_satellite_subpoint_lon` in the GLM files (GOES-East 75.2° W, GOES-West 137.0° W; a test
  checks the committed GOES-West file); coverage is a 67° central angle, the largest seen over 30
  days. Change them when NOAA moves or swaps a satellite, then bump `MASK_VERSION`.
- Stored mask: `data/contract/ownership.geojson`, one MultiPolygon per owner (split at the
  antimeridian), regenerated by `scintille-contract generate` and checked by `check`; meant for #37.
- At parse time, a flash whose point belongs to another instrument gets the quality flag
  `outside-owner-zone`. A point no configured instrument covers has no owner and no flag.
- The live feed skips flagged flashes ([hot path](hot-path.md#ingest-live-scintille-ingest-live)),
  so each flash is drawn once. Silver keeps both rows, so the choice can be revisited.
- Consumers that count flashes across sources (storm cells, storm tracks, intensity levels) must
  drop flagged rows: `NOT list_contains(quality, 'outside-owner-zone')`.
- MTG LI (#32) joins by adding its sub-point (0°) to `SATELLITES`: the mask then splits the
  Atlantic between GOES-East and MTG with no other change.

Limits: the mask is static, so while the owner of an area is down, its flashes from the other
satellite stay out of the live feed. Near the boundary the two copies of a flash can fall on
either side (both kept or both flagged); the band is a few km wide.

Measured on 30 days of GLM East + West in silver (between 2026-09-08 and 2026-10-08, 64.2 M
flashes): 13.4 M (20.9 %) are flagged, 9.75 M from GOES-West (74 % of its flashes) and 3.66 M from
GOES-East. On one day (2026-09-20), 91 % of flagged GOES-West flashes have a GOES-East flash within
0.5 s and 0.25°. For flagged GOES-East flashes the rate is 93 to 98 % over the storms of Mexico and
the western US, but near zero for clusters over the open south-east Pacific at the edge of GOES-
East's view, which GOES-West never reports (likely edge artefacts, which the live feed now also
skips).

## Asset checks on `silver.flashes` (Dagster)

| Check | When | Rule | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| `silver_freshness` | hourly | every source has flashes newer than 20 min | error |
| `silver_completeness` | hourly, last closed hour | ≥ 99 % of expected files (GLM 180/h, LI 120/h) | error |
| `silver_ranges` | hourly, last closed hour | lat/lon in range, energy > 0, event time within its product window | error |
| `silver_volume` | hourly, last closed hour | flash count within 0.2× to 5× the median of the same hour over 7 days | warning |
| `silver_uniqueness` | daily, yesterday (closed partitions only) | no `flash_id` twice; a violation calls for a partition rebuild (#40) | error |
| `silver_reconciliation` | daily, yesterday | per source and hour: files spooled in Postgres = files in bronze, flashes parsed = silver + quarantine | error |

- Error: something is wrong with the data or the pipeline and needs action. Error checks are
  `blocking`, so a failed result fails the run; a check that raises fails it too. Either way
  `alert_on_failure` posts it.
- Warning: unusual but possibly real (a quiet night, a huge storm); visible in the UI, no alert.

Reconciliation reads each side on its own (Postgres by the day encoded in the file name, bronze by
`product_start`, silver and quarantine by `product_file`) and lists the files missing from any side.

Schedules: `silver_hourly_checks` at :10 every hour, `silver_daily_checks` at 02:00 UTC. Results show
in the Dagster UI on the `silver/flashes` asset; their summary in `metrics.json` comes with #38.

Cost of uniqueness on a large partition: 4 million rows (about one day of one source) scan the
`flash_id` column and count distinct values in 0.15 s locally (0.05 s scan, 0.10 s count); on R2 the
scan is bounded by downloading that one column.
