# Assets

Globe textures are generated by `web/scripts/textures/build.mjs`, uploaded to R2, and never committed.

## Regenerate

```sh
brew install basis_universal   # KTX2 encoder (or set BASISU=/path/to/basisu)
pnpm -C web install
pnpm -C web textures
```

Sources are downloaded once to `web/scripts/textures/.cache/` and verified against a pinned size and
SHA-256. Outputs go to `web/scripts/textures/out/<tier>/` with a `manifest.json` listing every file size.
Both folders are git-ignored.

## Loading in the globe

The Earth shader loads `<PUBLIC_TEXTURES_URL>/4k/{day,night,mask}.webp`. `PUBLIC_TEXTURES_URL` defaults to
`/textures` in dev and to `https://data.scintille.app/textures/v2` in production builds. For local
development, link the generated folder into `public/` (ignored by Git):

```sh
ln -s ../scripts/textures/out web/public/textures
```

For production, upload them to R2 with `pnpm -C web r2:upload textures` ([deploy](deploy.md#textures)).

## Surface pyramid

The detail under the global textures ([ADR 0022](adr/0022-surface-imagery.md)): equirectangular
WebP tiles of 450 texels plus a one texel border, levels 2 to 5 by day, 2 to 4 by night. Built by
`web/scripts/textures/tiles.mjs`, checked by `check-tiles.mjs`, published to `textures/v3/` on R2.

```sh
cd web
node scripts/textures/tiles.mjs        # 588 MB of sources, about 3.5 min, out/surface/
node scripts/textures/check-tiles.mjs  # about 1.5 min, out/surface-check.json
pnpm r2:upload surface                 # tiles, then the manifest (deploy.md)
```

- **Sources**: the 8 Blue Marble Next Generation tiles (August 2004, topography without bathymetry)
  and the 8 Black Marble 2016 tiles at 15″, plus Natural Earth 10m ocean and lakes, pinned by size and
  SHA-256 in `tiles.mjs` and listed in the manifest with the attribution.
- **Tiles**: `{layer}/{z}/{x}-{y}.webp`, x from 180° W, y from the north pole. Day carries the water
  mask in a lossless alpha channel (255 water), colour kept under it. Borders repeat the neighbour's
  texels, across the antimeridian too; at a pole the border repeats the last row.
- **Left out**: tiles with nothing the global texture lacks (open water by day, unlit dark by night),
  unless a finer tile under them is kept. A missing tile means: draw the parent, or the global texture.
- **Manifest** (`manifest.json`, 12 kB): levels, a presence bitset per layer and level, byte counts,
  sources, attribution, encoder versions and a digest of every tile. The same sources and encoder
  versions give the same digest: two builds on 2026-10-28 gave identical manifests.

| Level | Tile | Texel | Day tiles | Day size | Night tiles | Night size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 15° | 3.7 km | 246 / 288 | 4.6 MB | 288 / 288 | 1.6 MB |
| 3 | 7.5° | 1.86 km | 813 / 1,152 | 14.9 MB | 1,035 / 1,152 | 5.1 MB |
| 4 | 3.75° | 0.93 km | 2,651 / 4,608 | 48.3 MB | 2,761 / 4,608 | 16.4 MB |
| 5 | 1.875° | 0.46 km | 9,115 / 18,432 | 138.0 MB | | |
| | | | | | **Total** | **16,909 files, 229.0 MB** |

Build on the reference Mac: 197 s with cached sources (day 2 min, night 25 s), 230 s with the
download, 8.9 GB peak memory.

### Checks

`check-tiles.mjs` fails on any of these, and `r2:upload surface` refuses a pyramid that has not passed:

- every listed tile exists, nothing else does, byte counts and digest match, and a kept tile's parent is kept;
- every border texel matches the texel it repeats, on 61,079 border lines: the water mask exactly, the
  colour within WebP noise (1 to 2 levels out of 255) and no worse at the antimeridian, at the seams
  between source files or at the poles than inside them;
- coastlines of the mask against the edges of the day colour and the night lights, on up to 240 coast
  tiles per level, peak within one texel;
- level 2 reduced to 4k against the published global textures: no shift (under 0.02 texel).

Measured: day and mask agree within half a 15″ pixel (the imagery sits about 230 m south of
Natural Earth, at every level); night within about one 15″ pixel, under its 742 m sensor pixel.
At the 400 km floor that is at most one CSS pixel.

### Costs

R2 prices of 2026-10 ([R2 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/)): storage
$0.015 per GB-month, writes $4.50 per million, reads $0.36 per million, no egress fee; 10 GB, 1 million
writes and 10 million reads free each month.

- Storage: 0.23 GB, $0.003 a month, inside the free tier.
- Publication: 16,910 writes, $0.08 outside the free tier, once per version.
- Transfer: a cold dive from `globe` to `iss` loads at most 0.92 MB desktop and 0.32 MB mobile (ADR 0022),
  about 50 tiles. Tiles are immutable and cached at the edge; even if every request reached R2, the
  free reads cover about 200,000 cold dives a month, then $0.36 per 20,000.

A rebuild with other content goes to a new version (`SURFACE_VERSION` in
`web/scripts/textures/surface.mjs` and `surfaceRoot` in `web/src/globe/earth.ts`); the upload refuses
to overwrite a published version with another digest.

## Sound

An ambient soundscape (#359): CC0 ambient tracks relayed in crossfades, and a short electric crackle for
flashes on screen. Built by `web/scripts/sound/build.mjs` (needs `ffmpeg` with libmp3lame) into
`web/public/sound/`, served with the site, and described by `web/src/globe/sound/catalog.json`.

```sh
pnpm -C web sound   # downloads the pinned Freesound previews once, about 20 MB
```

- **Sources**: Freesound HQ previews, all CC0, pinned by size and SHA-256 in `scripts/sound/sources.json`;
  titles, authors and links are in the catalog. No attribution is required.
- **Tracks**: a segment of at most 84 s per recording, normalized to -24 dBFS RMS, its last 4 s crossfaded
  into its start so it loops seamlessly, with 0.5 s of loop before and after so MP3 padding never reaches
  the loop points. MP3 96 kbps stereo, 0.4 to 1 MB each.
- **Crackles**: the 24 sharpest attacks of one static recording (a rise of more than 6 dB in 10 ms, at
  least 120 ms apart), 0.26 s each, band-passed 0.9 to 9 kHz. MP3 64 kbps mono, about 2 kB each.

Sound is off by default (browsers block audio until a user gesture) and turned on with the Sound switch.
The first track and the crackles load then; each next track loads 15 s before it fades in.

- A random track plays for 90 s, then the next one, never the same twice in a row, fades in over 8 s.
- Each flash crossing the playhead may get a crackle: one every 0.05 to 0.11 s at most (about 12 a
  second), the loudest flash crossed since. Volume follows intensity (to the power 1.5, so faint flashes
  stay in the background) and camera distance, a low-pass softens far flashes, and the playback rate
  varies from 0.8 to 1.25.
- Crackles are placed in 3D with an HRTF panner: the screen edges sound 80° to the side and 45° up or
  down, wider than the field of view. Best with headphones.
- Music and Flashes sliders under the Sound switch set each bus from 0 (muted) to full, squared; they
  are saved in `localStorage`. At most 16 crackles play at once; the mix goes through a gentle compressor. The audio context runs
  at 32 kHz when the browser allows it, which holds a decoded 84 s track in about 21 MB.

## Formats

Every tier has three equirectangular textures, each as KTX2 (Basis Universal ETC1S with a full mipmap
chain) and as a WebP fallback.

- `day.*`: sRGB color.
- `night.*`: sRGB city lights.
- `mask.*`: linear grayscale, water = white, land = black. Used for ocean specular.

KTX2 files are stored bottom-up (`-y_flip`) because Three.js cannot flip compressed textures: load them
with `flipY = false`. WebP files are top-down and use the default `flipY = true`. Both map onto
`SphereGeometry` UVs the same way.

| Tier | Size | KTX2 day / night / mask | KTX2 total | WebP total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `8k` | 8192 × 4096 | 2.67 / 1.43 / 0.42 | 4.53 | 3.37 |
| `4k` | 4096 × 2048 | 0.73 / 0.42 / 0.18 | 1.34 | 1.01 |
| `2k` (mobile) | 2048 × 1024 | 0.22 / 0.14 / 0.08 | 0.45 | 0.32 |

The mobile tier is well under the 4 MB target. Sizes change if encoder settings change: re-run the
script and update this table from `out/manifest.json`.
